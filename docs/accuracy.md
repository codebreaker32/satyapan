# Measured accuracy of the face and voice checks

Measured on public clips, never on customer data. We did not train or fine-tune either model; the thresholds were set on the genuine clips only. 'Held-out false alarms' counts genuine clips that did not help set the threshold (5-fold cross-validation). Fakes never set a threshold.

Outcomes are what the officer sees: flagged (counts in the score), unsure (not counted), no signs. Generated by `scripts/accuracy_card.py` from `calibration/*_runs.csv`.

## Where it fails (named)

- **face:** WhatsApp-size video (480 px or less): compression makes some genuine faces score like fakes, so the 5% false-alarm line sits at 0.97 and none of the 6 face swaps cleared it. On a WhatsApp forward the face check rarely flags; it says no signs or unsure.
- **face:** Real-world footage is often declined: 66 of 138 clips were not judged (frames disagree 24, face under 80 px 21, a usable face in too few frames 21). It says unsure rather than guess.
- **face:** Only 2 HD face swaps (one vendor sample), so the HD catch rate is an anecdote, not a measurement.
- **face:** Still images are not face-checked; they get the document check only.
- **face:** A video re-filmed with a camera pointed at a screen is not calibrated: the face check says unsure.
- **voice:** Less than 2 s of speech, or parts of a recording that disagree, and the voice check says unsure.
- **voice:** Voice conversion (FreeVC) is harder than text-to-speech (XTTS): on phone and WhatsApp-style Hindi audio it caught 20 and 23 of 31 FreeVC clones, against 29 of 29 XTTS clones.
- **voice:** A voice replayed through a speaker (recorded at the counter) hides traces of cloning: a clean result becomes unsure.
- **voice:** Genuine Hindi voices often score 0.99 or more on the raw model, so the Hindi lines sit at 0.9996 and above; a genuine video's sound track at 0.9986 landed in the grey band (unsure) in our live check.
- **both:** No public Indian scam-call audio or video exists to test on. Our fakes are lab-made (IndicSynth Hindi, In-the-Wild English celebrity speech, one face-swap vendor sample). Open detectors lose about half their accuracy on deepfakes circulated in 2024 (Deepfake-Eval-2024), so real-world catch rates may be lower.
