No model has been trained, fine-tuned or adapted by the team. No weights file is stored in this repository.

## Notes

- **Checkpoint state** uses the form's terms: off-the-shelf, LoRA, or custom fine-tune. Everything above is off-the-shelf or
  not yet loaded.
- **Why these detectors:** independent tests on deepfakes circulated in 2024 (Deepfake-Eval-2024, arXiv 2503.02857) show
  open-source detectors losing about half their accuracy in the wild, so we chose the models with the strongest published
  cross-dataset results and count them only through calibrated bands, never a raw score.
- **No training or fine-tuning.** Public datasets (listed in `README.md`) are used only to calibrate thresholds and
  measure accuracy. The data in `datasets/` and `prototype/data/` is synthetic, apart from the public domain registry
  answers recorded on 23 Oct 2026 in `prototype/data/mocks/web_registry.json`.
- **Forensics count through the combined score** (agreed by the team, 24 Oct; `prototype/satyapan/fusion.py`). With
  no media, or none a detector could judge, the instruction checks decide alone. With judged media:
  50% instruction + 50% deepfake, RED from 40%, AMBER from 15%. The deepfake % comes from the calibrated band (high
  100, moderate 70), never the raw score. A document's band is not calibrated: it comes from error-level analysis
  heuristics, editing-app names in the file's metadata and AI-provenance labels (`docs/changes-2026-10-24.md`).
  A RED still needs a money demand to have been read, and the officer decides.
  Wherever a forensic result is simulated it is labelled SIMULATED.
