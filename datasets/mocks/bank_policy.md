# Demo Bank: customer notice on "safe account" scams (bank policy document)

Published on the bank's website and printed in branches. The rulebook cites these lines.

- "We will never ask you to transfer money to a 'safe account'."
- "We will never ask you to break a fixed deposit or move your savings to another account to keep them safe."
- "Instructions about your money come only through the bank's official channels." Service calls come only from our 1600 number; our website ends in .bank.in.
- If anyone claiming to be from Demo Bank, the police, a court or any agency asks you to move money, stop and call 1600 123 4567 or visit your branch.
